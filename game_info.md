# Overwatch 2 Game Types and Rules

## 1. Introduction

Overwatch 2 is a team-based multiplayer first-person shooter developed by Blizzard Entertainment. This document provides an overview of the various game types available in Overwatch 2, along with their respective rules and player compositions.

## 2. Game Types in Overwatch 2

### 2.1 Quick Play

Quick Play is a fundamental game mode in Overwatch 2, offering a more relaxed experience compared to Competitive Play. It is divided into two distinct types: Open Queue and Role Lock.

#### 2.1.1 Open Queue
- In Open Queue, teams consist of 5 players with the freedom to choose any hero, regardless of role.
- This mode allows for flexible team compositions, encouraging creativity and experimentation.

#### 2.1.2 Role Lock
- Role Lock requires players to choose their role before the match, with each team comprising 1 Tank, 2 Damage, and 2 Support heroes.
- This mode ensures balanced team compositions, providing a structured gameplay experience.
